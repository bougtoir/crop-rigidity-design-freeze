# 11 — Novelty recheck (crop domain)

## Targeted searches (2026-10-03)

Topics swept: crop specialization × climate adaptation; crop diversity and
climate resilience; crop switching under climate change; crop migration;
agricultural lock-in; crop-system path dependence; specialization × shock
interaction; adaptation rigidity / rigidity traps / lock-ins.

## Closest prior work

1. **Costinot, Donaldson & Smith (2016, JPE)** — evolving comparative
   advantage over 1.7M fields (GAEZ): quantifies welfare gains *if* trade and
   production adjust; does **not** test whether pre-existing specialization
   predicts observed reallocation. Closest counterfactual cousin.
2. **Sloat et al. (2020) "Fruit basket upset"** — spatially explicit crop-mix
   *projections* under climate/economic pressure (modelled futures); not an
   observed-transformation test.
3. **Kurukulasuriya & Mendelsohn (2008)** — structural Ricardian crop
   switching in Africa: models choice under climate, but African-only and not
   framed as specialization → flexibility moderation.
4. **Crop-diversification lock-in literature** (e.g. goal-frames/cognitive
   lock-in, EU farm diversification pathways) — qualitative, regional,
   adoption-focused; no global quantitative interaction test.
5. Feasibility-phase Phase-1 matrix already established: no large-N
   Env×Legacy→transformation test exists in any domain (Carpenter & Brock
   2008; Allison & Hobbs 2004; Unruh 2000; Seto et al. 2016; Degroot et al.
   2021 are conceptual/framework/case work).

## Answers to the two critical questions

- *Has any prior global study tested whether pre-existing crop specialization
  modifies observed crop-system transformation under climatic mismatch?*
  **None found.** Existing work is modelled projection (Sloat), comparative-
  statics welfare (Costinot), regional Ricardian (Kurukulasuriya), or
  qualitative lock-in.
- *Has any prior global study quantified climate outcomes under inherited vs
  substitution-allowed allocation as a function of pre-existing
  specialization?* **None found**; the counterfactual module is genuinely
  adjacent to but distinct from GAEZ-based trade-adjustment exercises.

## Classification

Novelty remains **B (incremental but defensible)**: the mechanism is
conceptually anticipated, but the global observational interaction test and
the specialization-conditional counterfactual appear unoccupied. Do not claim
priority language ("first", "novel") in any output.
