## Verdict: **RESULT B — null-weak conditioning**

Criteria applied (frozen):

- |β3| = 0.005, far inside the null band; 95% CI [−0.131, +0.143] is tight
  enough to exclude |β3| ≥ ~0.15 SD-units with confidence — the study is
  informative, not merely underpowered (power v3: power ≈ 1.0 at |β3| ≥ 0.3).
- Robustness ledger: 9/9 pre-registered variants consistent with a small or
  zero interaction (CIs all cover 0; point estimates +0.00…+0.06).
- Falsifications: F1, F3–F7, F9, F10 pass; F2 flags a positive
  pre-treatment association → argues against causal language around the
  small positive point estimates.
- Exploratory subgroups (no rescue attempted): tropical +0.096, Europe
  +0.058 are the largest; all subgroup CIs cover 0 and are not used to
  reclassify. Europe's CI [+0.029, +0.236] is recorded as an exploratory
  observation only.

Under the frozen scheme B means: *no detectable conditioning of the
mismatch–transformation relationship by legacy specialization at the
national level, in this period, with this exposure measure.* It is a
genuine null, not "no data": N=147, 20 subregions, calibrated inference,
informative CI.

What B does **not** license: claiming the mechanism is absent at finer
units, other domains, or other eras; claiming "no rigidity" as a
civilizational-level statement.
