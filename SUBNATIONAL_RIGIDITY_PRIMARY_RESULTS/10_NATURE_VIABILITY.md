# 10 Nature-level gate

**NATURE PATH: NO-GO (for a Nature-track submission of this analysis)**

Across the gate criteria:

1. magnitude — Δ_ME ≈ −0.002 JSD, trivially small;
2. precision — CI tight around zero: informative, but a null;
3. spatial robustness — stable, except one prespecified vintage
   sensitivity (SPAM2010, disclosed);
4. geographic generality — multi-region, not global; Europe absent;
5. national-vs-subnational pattern — null at both scales, so the
   scale-dependent story is not supported;
6. H4 — redistribution is a minority channel (median 13%);
7. secondary fixed-capital evidence — null;
8. novelty — the question remains novel, but a precise null across two
   scales is unlikely to clear Nature editorial bar;
9. historical/non-agricultural validation — not present;
10. venue — Nature Sustainability / Nature Communications would face
    the same "two-scale null" objection.

The rigidity mechanism is simply not detectable in this design and
data. Any viable Nature-path would need a different data domain where
transformational events are actually observed — a redesign question,
not a reanalysis one.
