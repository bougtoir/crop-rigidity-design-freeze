# 00 — Executive decision

**DECISION: CONDITIONAL GO — GLOBAL SUBNATIONAL STUDY (admin-1 design)**

## What the audit found

1. **Novelty: real.** No global/multi-region empirical study has tested
   *pre-committed fixed agricultural capital × climatic mismatch → observed
   local transformation*. Existing work documents reallocation itself
   (Sloat et al. 2020), simulates it (crop-switching optimizations), or
   establishes fixed-capital mechanisms case-wise (irrigation institutions,
   perennial real options). The conditioning × decomposition design is new.

2. **The proposition can be tested — and a null is admissible.** Admin-1
   units with observed multi-vintage statistics (GSAP, Agro-MAPS, FAO
   subnational, SPAM input stats) provide a defensible outcome layer; the
   T = W + B decomposition makes H4 (aggregation escape) directly
   estimable and gives the completed national null a testable explanation.

3. **The condition — unit of analysis.** Pixel-level cross-vintage
   outcomes are REJECTED: SPAM vintages are not methodologically
   independent, and the pilot shows ~half the apparent pixel churn vanishes
   by 0.5°. The study must run on harmonized admin units fed by observed
   statistics, with modelled pixels used only for single-vintage baseline
   exposures (irrigation split, perennial share, crop footprint).

4. **Fixed capital beyond HHI exists.** Irrigation dependence
   (GMIA v5 / SPAM-`_I` / MIRCA — three readings) and perennial-crop share
   are physically interpretable, observable, and exactly the mechanism the
   programme needs ("spatially fixed legacy capital, not specialization
   per se").

## Binding risks carried into next phase

- Admin-boundary harmonization coverage is unquantified (the one audit
  item not yet executed — flagged, not hidden).
- F2-style pre-treatment contamination is a standing risk for any
  positive interaction (inherited lesson from the national run).
- Effect may again be null; that outcome is admissible and would itself
  complete the "rigidity is not generated by fixed local capital either"
  branch of the programme.
