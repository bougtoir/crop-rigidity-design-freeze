# 09 — Nature-level decision

**DECISION: CONDITIONAL GO**

## Can this study test a materially stronger proposition than the completed
national null?

Yes, and in two distinct ways, neither assumed true:

1. **H1–H3 (conditioning).** If spatially fixed capital is the operative
   legacy variable, a nonzero interaction should appear at the local unit
   even though the national portfolio interaction was null. The pilot
   confirms measurable subnational compositional change exists to be
   explained (~6–15% at admin scales).
2. **H4 (aggregation escape).** Even if every local unit shows rigidity,
   national JSD can remain high via between-unit reallocation. This is the
   more novel claim globally and it directly rationalizes the archived
   national null without re-opening it. The decomposition is estimable
   from the same observed stats — it does not depend on modelled pixels.

A null result (fixed local capital does NOT condition transformation)
remains scientifically informative: it would rule out the programme's
leading mechanistic rescue and redirect the programme toward
institutional/market channels.

## Why not GO unconditionally

- Coverage of harmonized admin units with ≥2 comparable vintages is
  plausible but unverified (GSAP 2010–21 vs an earlier vintage for
  baseline → baseline may need MRF/SPAM2000-era admin stats; window
  length vs GSAP's decade is a design tension).
- Boundary stability and method-change notes per unit must be audited
  before a lock can be written.
- Pixel-level route is off the table, so headline figures/maps will rely
  on admin units (less visually striking but honest).

## Nature-path assessment

The proposition "rigidity is generated by spatially fixed legacy capital
rather than aggregate specialization" is a genuinely stronger, mechanism-
level claim than the national test, and H4's decomposition is novel.
Conditional GO is justified now; full GO requires the admin coverage audit
and a second design-freeze (unit set, exposures A+B+C, decomposition,
inference — cluster bootstrap at country level, falsification suite
inherited and extended from the national package).
