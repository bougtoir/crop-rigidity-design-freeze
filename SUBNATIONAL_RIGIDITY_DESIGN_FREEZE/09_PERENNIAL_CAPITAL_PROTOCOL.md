# 09 — Perennial capital protocol (frozen)

Two nested definitions (column lifecycle in 04_CROP_CROSSWALK.csv):
- STRICT (perennial_woody, primary): coffee, tea, cocoa, oil palm, rubber,
  coconut, citrus, grapes, apples, mangoes, dates, cashew, olives,
  other_perennial, khat, sisal, spices-classified perennials.
- BROAD (perennial_woody + perennial_nonwoody, robustness): adds banana,
  plantain, pineapple, sugarcane.
Rationale: woody perennials carry multi-year irreversible sunk cost
(replanting lag) — the economic switching-cost concept; banana/sugarcane are
short-cycle resprouting perennials kept out of the primary to avoid
mechanical overlap with tropical crop mixes.
Exposure = baseline share of harvested area under the definition.
